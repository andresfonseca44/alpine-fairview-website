How much coverage do you actually need?

Most families fall into one of three groups. Find the one that sounds like you.

Cremation
$5,000 to $8,000
What it typically covers
  • The cremation and the funeral home’s basic services
  • Transportation and paperwork, like death certificates
  • An urn and a simple memorial or gathering
Why this range fits

Cremation usually costs less than a traditional burial. This range is often enough to cover the essentials without buying more coverage than you need.

Burial
$10,000 to $20,000
Why it costs more
  • A casket, burial plot and vault
  • A headstone or grave marker
  • Opening and closing the grave
  • A funeral or graveside service, flowers and transportation
Why we recommend this range

Burial has more separate costs, and they add up quickly. This range helps your family cover a full service without having to scramble for the money.

Leaving money to family
$25,000 to $50,000
What this level can do
  • Replace lost income for a period of time
  • Pay off debts or a mortgage
  • Cover final expenses
  • Leave a gift or legacy for children or grandchildren
Who it’s for

Choose this level if others depend on you financially, or if you would like to leave something behind.

Keep in mind: higher coverage amounts mean higher monthly costs. The goal isn’t the biggest number. It’s the amount that fits your family’s real need.

Product highlights

Here is what these policies offer, in plain language, and what each one means for your family.

Cash value

Part of your policy builds up savings over time. Depending on your policy, you may be able to borrow against it while you’re living. Any loan you don’t repay lowers what your family receives.

For your family: an extra source of money that is yours while you’re alive.

Accelerated death benefits

If you are diagnosed with a qualifying terminal illness, some policies let you use part of your benefit early. That money can help with care, bills, or time with the people you love. Availability and terms vary by policy.

For your family: it can lighten the load when they need it most.

Guaranteed acceptance, no medical exam

There is no medical exam. With guaranteed acceptance plans, your health history does not stand in the way of getting covered. Some of these plans have a waiting period before the full benefit is paid, and your agent will explain how it works for you.

For your family: coverage can be an option even when past health problems have made it hard to find elsewhere.

Fixed premiums that never increase

Your monthly cost is set when you start and stays the same for life. It does not go up as you get older or if your health changes.

For your family: a payment that is easier to plan around, especially on a fixed income.

Coverage that can’t be canceled

As long as your premiums are paid, the insurance company cannot cancel your coverage because of your age or your health.

For your family: the protection you set up is still there when they need it.
