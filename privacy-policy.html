Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Alpine Fairview Insurance Group ("Alpine Fairview", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, request whole life insurance rate quotes, or interact with our licensed advisors.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when completing our online lead generation quiz or requesting an instant quote, including:

  • Contact Details: First and last name, email address, mobile phone number.
  • Demographic & Eligibility Data: Date of birth, biological sex at birth, country and state of birth, US citizenship status.
  • Coverage Preferences: Desired Whole Life coverage amount ($5,000 to $50,000) and primary protection goals.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information collected to:

  • Calculate instant customized whole life insurance rate estimates.
  • Connect you with licensed Alpine Fairview insurance advisors to complete your application.
  • Submit carrier-required underwriting details to top-rated insurance providers on your behalf.
  • Send administrative notifications, policy confirmations, and customer service updates.

4. Information Sharing & Insurance Carriers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We may share relevant underwriting data only with trusted, A+ rated life insurance carrier partners strictly for the purpose of obtaining policy approvals and issuing your requested coverage.

5. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Your Rights & Opt-Out Preferences

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or opt out of receiving future marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at andres@alpinefairview.com.
