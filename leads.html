FAQs

Answers to common questions about Alpine Fairview leads.

What does "exclusive" mean? +

Each lead is delivered to one agent only. Exclusive leads are never resold or shared with other agents.

How are leads generated? +

Leads come from Meta video and ad campaigns run by Alpine Fairview. People interested in final expense coverage submit their information through our ad forms.

What close rate can I expect? +

Close rates generally range from 4% to 10%+ and depend heavily on the individual agent. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

Which states are available? +

Leads are available in all states.

Do I need to be licensed? +

Yes. Our leads are designed for licensed independent agents. Only contact leads in states where you hold an active license.

How do I set my daily volume? +

You choose your daily lead volume, with a minimum of 10 leads per day, and we handle everything else. Most veteran agents receive 20 leads per day. Let us know the volume you want during onboarding, or contact support@alpinefairview.com with any questions.

What happens after I complete the onboarding form? +

We use the information you provide to set up your lead delivery. Setup can take up to 48 hours. Once it is complete, leads are delivered to you as they are generated.

Why is delivery "within 48 hours" but leads are "immediate"? +

The 48 hours is the one-time setup after you complete onboarding. Once setup is done, each lead is delivered to you instantly when it is generated.

How do I pause or cancel my membership? +

Contact support@alpinefairview.com or call 773-800-0116 and we will take care of your request.

Are leads TCPA compliant? +

Leads opt in through our ad forms with consent language. Our consent language reads: By clicking "Get My Final Rate", you consent to receive rate quote details and policy updates from Alpine Fairview Group at the phone number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
